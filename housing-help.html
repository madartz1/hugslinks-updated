NYC Housing Programs

These are some of the major housing and rental-assistance programs available to New Yorkers.

Rental Assistance

CityFHEPS

CityFHEPS is a New York City rental-assistance program that may help eligible households leave shelter or avoid entering shelter.

  • May help with ongoing rent
  • Used by eligible households searching for apartments
  • Eligibility and documentation rules apply
  • Some households may work through shelter staff or Homebase

Eviction Prevention

Homebase

Homebase assists eligible New Yorkers who are at risk of entering shelter and want to remain stably housed in their community.

  • Eviction prevention
  • Emergency rental assistance
  • Benefits assistance
  • Financial counseling
  • Relocation assistance

If You Are In Shelter

Ask about every housing pathway you may qualify for.

Depending on your household and circumstances, rental-assistance programs may include CityFHEPS, FHEPS, Pathway Home, SOTA, HUD-VASH for qualifying veterans, or supportive housing.

Keep copies of documents you submit and ask for written confirmation when important paperwork is completed or transmitted.

Housing Preparation

Keep your documents together.

Housing programs often require documentation. Having copies ready can make applications and apartment searches easier.

Apartment Search

Searching for an apartment with assistance

A housing search can involve more than finding a listing. Before committing money or submitting sensitive information, make sure you understand the apartment, landlord or broker, program requirements and any required inspection or approval.

1. Know your budget Understand the rent level your household or subsidy can support.
2. Verify the listing Confirm the apartment and person offering it are legitimate.
3. Keep your documents ready Save digital and paper copies of important housing paperwork.
4. Get required approval Voucher or subsidy apartments may require program review and inspection before move-in.
Affordable Housing

Apply even while pursuing other options.

Housing Connect applications can take time, so affordable-housing lotteries should usually be viewed as one part of a larger housing search rather than the only option.

Opportunities can have different income, household-size and eligibility requirements.

Housing Search Tip

Look beyond lotteries.

Affordable-housing resources can also include re-rentals, public housing, Mitchell-Lama developments, supportive housing and rental-assistance programs.

Housing Update · 2026

Important program information changes over time.

NYCHA's general Section 8 waiting list is currently closed to new applicants. Existing applicants should continue checking their application status and keeping contact information current.

Mitchell-Lama housing opportunities are also changing: current lotteries are now being handled through NYC Housing Connect rather than the former Mitchell-Lama Connect portal.

Housing Rights

Your housing assistance should not automatically disqualify you from an apartment.

New York City housing protections may apply when landlords, brokers or others refuse to consider lawful sources of income such as qualifying rental subsidies or vouchers.

If you believe you are experiencing housing discrimination, improper fees, dangerous conditions or another housing problem, use official housing and legal resources to understand your options.

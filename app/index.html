How monthly giveaways work

Drawings are planned for the end of each month. Each promotion will list its exact entry deadline, drawing date, confirmed gifts, and official rules. Enter through the public free form when that month’s promotion opens, with one entry per eligible person. A random drawing will select a potential winner. Attendance, payment, and marketing signup will not be required. Prize types and values vary by month.

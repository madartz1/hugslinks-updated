Diet-friendly food

Use fresh-food markers for produce and EBT-friendly shopping, then use the trusted resource links below for free meals and groceries. Vegetarian availability is explicitly listed by some providers; vegan and pescatarian availability should be confirmed with each location.

Healthy & dietary food paths

Vegetarian

Prioritize farmers markets, produce distributions and meal programs that publish vegetarian choices. Confirm current menu availability before traveling.

Vegan

Use the fresh-food and EBT filters for produce-focused options. For prepared meals, contact the provider because ingredients and vegan availability can change by distribution.

Pescatarian

Use fresh-food markets and grocery assistance for produce and pantry staples, and confirm seafood availability directly with participating food programs or stores.

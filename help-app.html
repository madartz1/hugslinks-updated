HUG Requests

What can you call on HUGS for?

HUGS Help focuses on practical community needs where connection, information and coordinated assistance can make a meaningful difference.

🥗 Food Support

Request or locate available food, prepared meals and participating community food resources.

🛍️ Essential Shopping

Request assistance obtaining basic groceries or essential household items when available.

📦 Supply Delivery

Connect available supplies with people or approved community projects that can use them.

🏪 Partner Pickup

Coordinate approved contributions from participating businesses to their destination.

🏠 Housing Navigation

Connect with HUGSLinks housing information, official resources and navigation tools.

📋 Resource Navigation

Find government, nonprofit, community and support resources from one easier starting point.

🎨 Creative Support

Connect community projects with artists, creators and useful creative skills.

💼 Business Support

Connect entrepreneurs with information, services and opportunities through HUGSLinks.

How a HUG Mission Works

From request to HUG Delivered.

HUGS Help uses a managed mission process. Requests can be reviewed before becoming available HUG Missions, helping protect recipients and Helpers while the community network grows.

1
Request Someone submits a HUG Request.
2
Review HUGS reviews and organizes the request.
3
Match An approved mission can be matched with an approved Helper.
4
Help The Helper completes the approved pickup, delivery or assistance.
5
Confirm The completed mission becomes HUG Delivered.

HUG Delivered.

The goal isn't simply to collect requests. It is to create a visible path from need → connection → action → impact. Completed missions can help show what the community accomplished while protecting private personal information.

HUGS Help is a community-support service and is not an emergency service. Requests and Helper participation may require review and approval before a connection or mission is made. Do not submit passwords, Social Security numbers, banking credentials or other unnecessary highly sensitive information. If you are experiencing an immediate emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency service.

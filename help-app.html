HUG Requests

What could you call on HUGS for?

HUGS Help will begin with practical, community-level needs where connection and coordination can make a meaningful difference.

🥗 Food Pickup

Coordinate pickup of available food, prepared meals or approved food support.

🛍️ Essential Shopping

Help obtaining basic groceries or essential household items when appropriate.

📦 Supply Delivery

Connect available supplies with people or community projects that can use them.

🏪 Partner Pickup

Help move approved contributions from participating businesses to their destination.

🏠 Housing Navigation

Connect to HUGSLinks housing information, official resources and navigation tools.

📋 Resource Navigation

Find government, community and support resources in one easier starting place.

🎨 Creative Support

Connect community projects with artists, creators and useful creative skills.

💼 Business Support

Connect entrepreneurs with information, services and opportunities through HUGSLinks.

How a HUG Mission Works

From a request to a delivered HUG.

The first HUGS Help system will use a managed process. Requests can be reviewed before they become available HUG Missions. That helps HUGSLinks grow the service responsibly before adding more advanced dispatch technology.

1
Request Someone submits a HUG Request.
2
Review HUGS reviews and organizes the request.
3
Match An approved mission becomes available to a Helper.
4
Help The Helper completes the approved assistance.
5
Confirm The completed mission becomes a HUG Delivered.

HUG Delivered.

The goal isn't simply to collect requests. It is to create a visible path from need → connection → action → impact. As HUGS Help grows, completed missions can help show the community what was accomplished while protecting private personal information.

HUGS Help is being developed in phases. During the initial phase, requests and Helper participation may require manual review before a connection is made. Do not submit passwords, Social Security numbers, banking information or other highly sensitive information through general contact forms. HUGS Help is not an emergency service. For an immediate emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency service.

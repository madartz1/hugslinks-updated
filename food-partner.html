How it works

HUGSLinks reviews each offer before coordinating a pickup or food-support mission.

  1. Submit your food or funding offer.
  2. We review the details and pickup window.
  3. HUGSLinks confirms next steps with your contact person.
  4. Approved food is coordinated for community support.
Food safety matters.
Please provide accurate preparation, storage and allergen information. Submission does not guarantee that HUGSLinks can accept or pick up an offer.

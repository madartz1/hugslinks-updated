Supporter rewards & project participation

Where HUGSLinks has made a specific agreement with an individual or business concerning financial support, rewards, benefits, project participation or proceeds, that agreement remains the reference for what was promised. First Supporter recognition may also include access to eligible HUGS programs, projects, products, art and gallery initiatives as they become available.

Member status by itself does not create an investment, ownership interest, guaranteed return, revenue share or entitlement to proceeds. Any financial participation or compensation applies only where separately agreed and documented. Future programs may have their own terms and eligibility requirements.

HUGSLinks • B2B Food Partnerships

Start a food partnership conversation.

Restaurants, grocers, caterers, markets, food-service businesses and community organizations can contact HUGSLinks about supporting food access in New York City. Public submissions are inquiries only. Donation quantities, pickup locations, timing and operational coordination are handled privately by HUGSLinks administration after review.

Business inquiry

Tell us who you are and what kind of partnership you want to discuss. Do not enter pickup instructions, private recipient details or time-sensitive food handling information here.

Private coordination: If HUGSLinks decides to move forward, donation and pickup details will be handled through the private administrative workflow—not on the public resource map.

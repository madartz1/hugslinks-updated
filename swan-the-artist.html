About this collection

The Luxe Collection

The Luxe Collection brings Swan’s artwork into a conversation with the spaces around it. Gold tones, dramatic contrast and layered surfaces give the collection its visual character.

Its launch work, Golden Horizon, presents a cityscape in black and gold, with a luminous sky and reflections across the water. The skyline’s energy and the warmth of its palette invite viewers to consider both the image and the atmosphere it creates.

Explore the interactive room displays to compare Golden Horizon with Swan — The Eye across living rooms, dining spaces, hotel interiors and an executive office.

The Black Gold Series

The Black Gold Series explores observation, transformation, identity, energy and depth. In its launch work, Swan — The Eye, a gold swan emerges from a dark field, with a bright point of light at its eye and expressive gold marks around it.

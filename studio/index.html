THE DIRECTOR’S DESK15-second commercial

From idea to finished ad.

Choose a story, add your visuals, and bring it together with your brand and soundtrack.

01 / Choose your direction

One audience. One main message. One action. Lead with your strongest visual, show a clear benefit, then make the next step easy.

02 / Build your four-scene story

Select a scene to edit it. Move scenes earlier or later, set each scene to 1–10 seconds, and choose a video’s start and end points. Short clips hold their final frame for the rest of the scene. Original video audio is muted.

Total: 15 seconds

03 / Make it yours

Upload your logo to brand each scene and the closing card.

Design your ad

Choose a visual style and typography. Changes appear in the preview and carry through to your video.

Fonts use the closest available device font. Check all four scenes before exporting.
♫ Music & optional narrationUpload your recorded voiceover. Music loops to fill 15 seconds; narration plays once from the beginning. AI voice generation and automatic speech captions are not included yet.

04 / Review your commercial

Full HD keeps text and logos crisp. Source photos and AI footage retain their original level of detail. If your phone struggles with Full HD, select HD or Compact.

Review all four text overlays. Keep each message short and confirm that the final call to action matches your goal.

Preview and assembly use your device and do not consume AI generations. Keep this tab open. Export is MP4 when your browser supports it, otherwise WebM. Saving the script keeps scene order and timing, but not attached media or video trims; reattach files and set trims after returning.

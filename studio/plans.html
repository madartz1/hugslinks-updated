Credit terms

When do credits renew?

On your subscription billing renewal date. Unused credits expire at the end of each billing cycle; they do not roll over.

Do retries and revisions count?

Yes, a successful new generated output or revision counts. A failed generation should not consume credits. Successful duplicate requests must not be charged twice when retried with the same request identifier.

What happens when I use them all?

Generation is blocked before another paid API call. You can wait for renewal or change to an eligible plan. Extra credit packs will be offered only once pricing and purchasing are implemented.

Can I see my balance?

Credit balance and dated generation history will appear in the authenticated member dashboard when that feature is connected. No account balance is shown on this preview page.

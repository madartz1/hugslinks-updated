Built From Experience

Turning hardship into something constructive.

The people building HUGSLinks are entrepreneurs who are familiar with experiencing hardship, instability, barriers and injustice firsthand.

Instead of allowing those experiences to end with hardship, HUGSLinks is being developed as a way to transform experience into creativity, education, community connection, practical resources and opportunities for people to help one another.

A purchase on this page immediately supports the independent entrepreneurs doing this work and helps us continue developing the larger HUGSLinks mission.
